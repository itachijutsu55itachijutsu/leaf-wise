## How this started

Caring for houseplants at home has shown me how confusing it can be when two plants develop similar symptoms but need different care. In my [winter houseplant care guide](/winter-houseplant-care/), I describe how a money plant and an aloe vera I had kept for four years developed yellowing leaves, and how I sought advice from a nursery specialist. Both plants began recovering after I followed the advice. The exact cause was not established.

## What {{name}} is for

{{name}} is a small, independent site that explains plant care in plain language, so beginners can diagnose common problems and choose appropriate next steps without wading through filler. Each guide focuses on one question, like how often to water, why leaves turn yellow, or when to repot.

## How the guides are written

- Guides draw on cited horticultural references and clearly identified firsthand experiences. Illustrative scenarios are examples, not claims of personal results.
- I try to explain why something works, not just tell you what to do.
- I update guides when I learn something better, and I fix mistakes when readers point them out.
- I'm not a professional horticulturist, so for serious problems or pet safety questions, please check with a qualified expert or your vet.

## Advertising and honesty

This site may show advertisements to cover hosting costs. Advertising is separate from editorial guidance. If I ever add affiliate links or sponsored content, I'll label it clearly.
