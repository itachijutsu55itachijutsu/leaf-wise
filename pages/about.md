## How this started

I didn't begin as a plant expert. Like most beginners, I bought a few houseplants because they made my room feel calmer, and then I almost killed them. I watered on a fixed schedule, left plants in the wrong light, and panicked every time a leaf turned yellow. Searching online gave me conflicting advice, long articles that never answered the actual question, and lots of jargon.

Slowly I started paying attention instead of following rules: checking the soil with my finger, noticing which window gave which kind of light, and changing one thing at a time. My plants got healthier, and I realized the problem was never that plant care is hard. It's that most advice is explained badly.

## What {{name}} is for

{{name}} is a small, independent site where I write down what I've learned in plain language, so a beginner can solve a problem in a few minutes without wading through filler. Each guide focuses on one question, like how often to water, why leaves turn yellow, or when to repot.

## How the guides are written

- They're based on established horticultural advice and on my own experience growing plants at home.
- I try to explain why something works, not just tell you what to do.
- I update guides when I learn something better, and I fix mistakes when readers point them out.
- I'm not a professional horticulturist, so for serious problems or pet safety questions, please check with a qualified expert or your vet.

## Advertising and honesty

This site may show advertisements to cover hosting costs. Ads never change what I write, and I only recommend things I would use myself. If I ever add affiliate links or sponsored content, I'll label it clearly.
